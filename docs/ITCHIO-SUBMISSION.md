# 🪙 Coin Empire

**Build your business empire!** Start with a lemonade stand, grow to a galactic corporation, and become the ultimate tycoon.

## Features
- 🍋 **8 Businesses** — From Lemonade Stand to Galaxy Inc
- 📈 **14 Upgrades** — Boost your click power and income
- 🏢 **Prestige System** — Go Public for permanent multipliers
- 👔 **Managers** — Automate your empire
- 🎁 **Daily Rewards** — 7-day streak bonuses
- 💤 **Offline Earnings** — Earn while you're away
- 📱 **Mobile Friendly** — Works great on phones
- 🔊 **Sound Effects** — Satisfying clicks and chimes
- 📤 **Share** — Challenge your friends

## How to Play
1. **Tap** the coin to earn
2. **Buy** businesses to generate income
3. **Hire** managers to automate
4. **Upgrade** for bigger profits
5. **Go Public** (prestige) for permanent boosts
6. **Come back daily** for streak rewards!
